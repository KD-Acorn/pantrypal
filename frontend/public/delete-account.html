Delete Your Account

Effective: September 12, 2026 · Last updated: September 12, 2026

This page explains how to delete your My Pantry Club account and what happens to your data when you do. You do not need to contact us to delete your account — it's a self-service action inside the app.

1. How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open My Pantry Club and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the request.

That's it — no account deletion request needs to be emailed or submitted anywhere else.

2. The 7-Day Grace Period

Once you request deletion, your account is scheduled for permanent deletion 7 days later — it is not deleted immediately. During those 7 days:

  • You'll see a countdown screen when you open the app.
  • You can tap Keep My Account at any time to cancel the deletion and keep your account exactly as it was.
  • You can instead tap Delete Everything Now to skip the waiting period and delete immediately.

If you take no action, everything below is permanently deleted automatically once the 7 days are up.

3. What Gets Deleted

When deletion completes (either after 7 days or immediately, if you chose to skip the wait), the following is permanently and irreversibly removed:

  • Your pantry and all ingredients
  • Your saved recipes and cook history
  • Your grocery list and meal plans
  • Your login credentials and account record (your Firebase Auth account is deleted)

4. What Is Retained

  • Community recipes you shared publicly are kept, but anonymized — your name and account are stripped and replaced with "Community Member." They are not deleted, since other users may have saved or be relying on them.
  • If you owned a shared household, ownership automatically transfers to the next co-admin (in order of who joined first) so the household and its members are unaffected. If no co-admin exists, the household is disbanded.
  • We may retain anonymized, aggregated analytics that can no longer be tied to you.

We do not retain your email address, pantry contents, recipes, or any other personally identifiable data once deletion completes.

5. Questions

If you have questions about account deletion or what happens to your data, contact us at: privacy@mypantryclub.com
